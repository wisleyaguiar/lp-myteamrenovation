Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

My Team Renovation (“My Team Renovation”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a shower and bathroom renovation company serving Katy, Fulshear, Richmond and the greater Houston area, Texas. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a free estimate, we collect your first name, mobile phone number, ZIP code, the type of project you are interested in and, if you choose to provide it, your email address.

Information collected automatically. When you visit this page we receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, the date and time of your visit, and how you reached us (for example, the ad or link you clicked, including campaign tags such as UTM parameters and ad click IDs).

2. How we use your information

  • To contact you by phone call, text message or email about your estimate and your project.
  • To schedule, prepare and deliver your estimate and any work you hire us to do.
  • To keep records of our conversations and of your request.
  • To measure and improve our advertising and this website.
  • To prevent spam and abuse, and to comply with the law.

3. Text messages and calls

By submitting the estimate form, you agree that My Team Renovation may call you and send you text messages about your estimate and project at the number you provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help.

We do not share or sell your mobile phone number or text messaging opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Cookies, pixels and advertising

This website uses the Meta Pixel and the Meta Conversions API to understand how people find us through Facebook and Instagram ads and to measure whether those ads lead to estimate requests. These tools may set cookies in your browser and receive information such as your IP address, browser details, pages viewed and actions taken on this site. When you submit the form, we may send Meta a hashed (encrypted, one-way) version of your phone number, first name, ZIP code and email so Meta can match the request to an ad. We may also use Google Analytics to see how visitors use the site.

You can control cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage how Meta uses your data for ads in your Facebook or Instagram Ad Preferences. To opt out of Google Analytics, you can use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as our customer relationship management (CRM) system, website hosting, phone and text messaging providers. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Advertising and analytics partners (Meta and Google), as described in section 4.
  • Authorities, when required by law, or to protect our rights, our customers or the public.
  • A successor business, if My Team Renovation is ever merged, sold or reorganized.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to your request, serve you as a customer, and meet our legal, tax and warranty obligations. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. How we protect it

This website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and access to customer information is limited to people who need it to do their job. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what personal information we have about you, or give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • stop contacting you (or reply STOP to any text);
  • stop using your information for targeted advertising.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 45 days and may need to confirm your identity first. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and if you are still not satisfied you may contact the Texas Attorney General.

9. Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
